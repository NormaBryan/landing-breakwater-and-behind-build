STUDENT PROJECT · CASE F

Behind the Build

Breakwater — From an idea to a working surf school app

Atlantic Edge Surf School · Lahinch, Co. Clare

Task 1 — Think

My original idea before using AI

It could be used for different outdoor activities that are seasonal or weather dependant like horse riding, kayaking or boat trips.

The niche I chose

I decided to focus on small independent surf schools along Ireland's Wild Atlantic Way. These businesses depend heavily on weather conditions and often need to rearrange lessons at short notice.

Who would use it?

The buyer would be the surf school owner or manager. The daily user could be the owner, receptionist or instructor responsible for organising lessons.

The problem

In the Atlantic Edge case, Niamh uses a paper diary, telephone calls and WhatsApp to manage bookings. When lessons are cancelled, she spends about three hours rearranging customers. Around one in five cancelled bookings never rebooks.

Customer voice

"When the weather changes, I have to contact everyone and try to find another lesson. It takes hours."

"If customers don't rebook, I lose money. I need to know who is waiting and where I have spaces."

These are illustrative customer statements, not quotes from real interviews.

Competitor research

I looked at existing booking services including eola and SimplyBook.me. They offer booking and scheduling features. My idea focuses more narrowly on managing weather-related cancellations and rebookings.

Proposed price

€99 one-off setup fee and €49 per month. This is an assumption for the project and has not been tested with paying customers.

Lean Product Requirements

Hypothesis: Small surf schools may pay for easier weather-related rebooking.

Audience: Independent Irish surf school owners and managers.

Main action: Join the waiting list.

Success measure: In a future live version, measure genuine sign-ups and interest from surf school owners.

Not included: Payments, live weather forecasts, automatic messages or customer self-booking.

Task 2 — Alpha Test

I used AI to help build Breakwater as a single HTML file containing HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The app uses fictional sample bookings and has Today, Rebook, Money and Calendar views.

Test 1 — Cancelling a lesson

What I tried: I selected a lesson and tried to cancel it.

What went wrong: The cancellation button was not working properly.

What I changed: I asked AI to help correct the cancellation function. It took several attempts to get it working.

Result: I tested it again and was able to cancel a lesson. The affected customers moved into Rebook and the Money section updated.

Test 2 — Future bookings

What confused me: I could see today's lessons but not the future dates where customers had been rebooked.

What I changed: I asked AI to add a Calendar showing future lesson dates, times and customer names.

Result: After rebooking a customer, I could see the customer on the new date in the Calendar. I tested this and confirmed it worked.

Test 3 — Testing on my phone

After I corrected the problems on my computer, I downloaded the updated app and opened it on my phone. I was able to use the corrected version on my phone as well.

What I learned

I learned that just because AI creates something does not mean it will work correctly the first time. I had to test the buttons, explain the problems and ask for corrections. I also realised that seeing future bookings was important for the person using the app. Testing helped me improve the final version.

Further checks

I have not separately recorded a phone-browser refresh test or a full capacity-limit test. The app is still a prototype using sample data. It does not send messages or take payments.

Task 3 — Sell

The product

I named my product Breakwater. It is aimed at small independent Irish surf schools that need a simpler way to deal with cancelled lessons.

The promise

Less chasing. More surfing.

How it works

Step 1: Cancel a lesson and move affected customers into the rebooking queue.

Step 2: Find another suitable lesson with enough available places.

Step 3: Check who is still waiting and the potential money at risk.

Pricing

I proposed €49 per month and a €99 setup fee. This is not a live commercial offer.

Waiting list

The landing page includes a demonstration waiting-list form. It displays a thank-you message but does not collect, save or send personal information.

Limitations

This is a student prototype rather than a finished commercial product. The bookings are fictional, and the app does not include real customer messaging, payments or live weather information.
